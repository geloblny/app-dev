# Hunger Games

The Hunger Games franchise expands beyond the original 2008 novel through two direct sequels that conclude Katniss Everdeen's story, alongside two prequel novels that explore Panem's history. Author Suzanne Collins crafted these follow-ups to examine rebellion, media propaganda, and authoritarian control.

_**Direct Sequels to the Original Story**_

Following the events of the 74th Hunger Games, the narrative continues across two main installments:
**1. Catching Fire (Book: 2009 | Film: 2013)**
	• The Premise. Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark embark on a mandatory Victory Tour while unrest spreads across the districts.
	• The Twist. President Snow announces the Third Quarter Quell, forcing past victors back into a deadly clock-shaped arena.
**2. Mockingjay (Book: 2010 | Films: Part 1 in 2014, Part 2 in 2015)**
	• The Premise. Rescued from the arena, Katniss relocates to the underground bunker of District 13 and becomes the reluctant symbol of a full-scale revolution.
	• The Climax. Rebels march on the Capitol to overthrow President Snow's regime, leading to high personal sacrifices and a reshaped Panem.
"If we burn, you burn with us!" — Katniss Everdeen, Mockingjay
